# Data policy

Project: **What OCR Evaluation Reveals and Conceals: Reference Integrity, Text Representation, and Retrieval Diagnostics for Printed Kazakh Book OCR**

## Included material

Aggregate edit counts, denominators, scores, resampling summaries, section/page correspondence labels, source hashes, numeric alignment offsets and status qualifications are included. The paired representation result contains numeric counts and codepoint diagnostics. Short ordinal labels appear in code and the failure trace. Full literary passages are excluded.

The records preserve their original values and scientific qualifications. S is defective, D is provisional, and the separate C proposal is unadjudicated and absent. B measurements express agreement with a digital reference. Legacy retrieval metrics are diagnostic-only. Synthetic fixtures are software tests.

## Excluded material

The package includes no book scans, page images, full references/transcriptions, complete OCR/correction outputs, author photographs, private review evidence, source notebooks, historical conference materials, model weights or installed dependency trees.

The only PDFs are the three approved vector figures. The only PNGs are their three README previews. The only font binaries are the two approved Noto faces. No ZIP or other distributable archive is included.

## Permission and access

Study-use permissions do not establish public redistribution permission for the source materials. No external access arrangement is active or promised by this package. See SOURCE_ACCESS.md.

Aggregate compilation and authored artifact licensing remain unresolved. No MIT or Creative Commons license is applied by this document. Third-party font terms are preserved separately.

Citation metadata use the six approved author names and ORCIDs. Personal email addresses, author portraits and private reviewer information are excluded.
